        Head-pet tuning

        Easiest: tap Calibrate, stay quiet 3 s, then rub the head for 3 s. Manual: rub energy must exceed the floor, exceed ratio × baseline, and flatness must exceed its minimum.

        Belly scratch tuning

        Easiest: tap Calibrate, stay quiet 3 s, then scratch for 3 s. It sets the floor and ratio from what it heard.
